# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, React, CSS, dnd-kit, optional Supabase. Chosen from the user's preferred stack.

## Users

Indonesian-speaking SMP and SMA students with limited access to physical science laboratories; teachers preparing guided science activities.

## Product Purpose

Help students learn science by performing guided virtual experiments, observing a result, and explaining it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Chemistry, physics, and biology laboratories; nine seeded experiments; three flagship simulations; step validation, accessible placement alternative, quizzes, progress, and teacher assignments. Simulations teach focused concepts rather than model all physical conditions.

## Product Principles

- Make the action and its consequence visible.
- Explain mistakes with helpful feedback.
- Keep the science inspectable and the interface calm.
